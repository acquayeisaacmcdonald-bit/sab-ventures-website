    Our story

    Wholesale prices, retail patience.

    SAB Ventures started as a small stall by Kokoase Taxi Station and has grown into Bogoso's go-to shop for bulk packaging — polythene bags, disposables, bottles and household supplies. Maame Serwaah still deals with customers personally, whether it's a single bag of ice-water rubber or a wholesale order for a whole shop.
